## 1. Verdict

**The plumbing is correct.** This is verified, not inferred:
- An independent H1 ledger matches all 23 trades exactly (net difference ≤ 7.9e-11). `run_all.main(False)` reproduces all 7 result files byte for byte.
- A direct re-read of the archive matches the cache with a maximum difference of 0.0. The feature CSV reproduces from code with a maximum relative difference of 5e-6.
- Settlement dates come from `ts_ref` in 24,179 of 24,179 records. Preliminary and final settlements are identical in all 7,434 overlapping cases.
- The code has no lookahead (`run_all.py:84`, `src/backtest.py:58,90`).

**Three bugs change numbers. None changes the H1 verdict.**
1. **Commercial gas use is silently dropped from the demand weights.** This deviates from PREREGISTRATION §3 (line 47). It changes 1 of 23 trades. H1 Sharpe moves from 0.073 to 0.105 at 1x costs and from 0.008 to 0.041 at 2x.
2. **P1 compares calendar-day signal changes with session-to-session returns.** This is your known issue (ii). It changes the "is it priced?" answer: once aligned, mean revisions are clearly priced, and ΔB is not robust.
3. **`drop_episodes` also zeroes the last day of the neighbouring trade** (`src/analysis.py:61`). Leave-out-Elliott Sharpe should be 0.147, not the reported 0.179. Leave-out-Jan-2025 should be 0.094, not 0.088.

**Two design flaws, both faithful to the pre-registration:**
- **The trigger lags the market.** It fires after the spread has already moved, so the trade enters after the information is priced.
- **The tail threshold is set by the forecast's start month,** which creates a calendar sawtooth. Two of the 23 trades exist only because of it.

**Acceptable limitations to disclose:**
- The forecast window falls in a delivery month other than the traded contract's. This is structural to monthly futures with a 5-session hold.
- The rule trades the level of p_cold, by design.
- Only 00Z runs are available.
- Sizing σ (your known issue i) is immaterial.
- K is noisy: reliability 0.644.
- Gas day 9 has a warm bias of −0.044 HDD in Hbar.
- Leave-one-winter-out thresholds are not real-time.
- Newey-West with 5 lags is oversized: about 10% rejection at a nominal 5%.
- The bootstrap confidence interval depends on block length.

**Bottom line.** "H1 not supported" stands. The right wording is: **an inconclusive, underpowered null, dependent on one storm, in which all four pre-registered falsifiers (§2, line 39) fire in development:**

| Falsifier | Evidence |
|---|---|
| No increment beyond the mean | M2 p_cold t = −0.70; 95% CI [−0.35, +0.17] in SD units |
| Edge confined to one storm | Uri trades net $23,120, more than the $14,150 total; the other 21 trades net −$8,970 |
| Gross edge removed by 2× costs | 2× costs are $25,100, 94% of the $26,700 gross; Sharpe 0.008 |
| Effect only before entry | Spread up +1.04σ over D−5..D−1 at onsets (p = 0.005), then −0.04σ over D→D+5 against a +0.31σ base rate |

**Audit claims that did not survive verification. Do not put these in the note:**
- "Two hypotheses that cannot both be true" (economics-04) was refuted. Tail-neglect underreaction is compatible with a level rule.
- "The expiry buffer causes the delivery mismatch" is wrong. Of the 175 M2–M3 sessions, 125 are forced by the 5-session hold. Even with a buffer of 0, overlap is only 0.030.
- "Three threshold-artifact trades" should be 2.
- "The ±15-day threshold keeps 16 of 23 trades" is wrong. A path-dependent rerun keeps 11 of 23, with Sharpe −0.19.
- "Δa does not survive (t ≈ 0.7)" is an artifact. That spec drops Mondays and tests the wrong day on Tue–Fri; see issue 2.
- "p_cold t = −3.01 excluding Feb 2021" is cherry-picked. The h = 2/3/5 numbers use pair(t,15).
- "H1 trails B0 on every measure" is false at 2× costs: 0.008 vs −0.090.
- "M2 needs partial R² 0.123 to be detectable" is wrong. The corrected figure is 0.050.

## 2. Surviving issues, ranked

**1. The trigger lags the market: the spread move is priced before entry.** Design flaw, verified.
- **Evidence (timing-01):**
  - At H1 onsets (n = 21), the spread rose +1.043σ over D−5..D−1. Median +0.61σ, 81% positive, permutation p = 0.005. Month-matched p = 0.0075; winter-matched p = 0.0003.
  - On non-overlapping events (n = 17), the sign test gives p = 0.013.
  - On the onset day itself (D−1→D) the move is −0.05σ. Over the hold (D→D+5) it is −0.04σ, against +0.31σ for all sessions.
  - Lead-lag: a_z predicts returns on sessions k = −4..0 with t from +2.2 to +4.6, and on k = +1..+5 with t from −0.07 to −1.13.
  - Pre-window regression: a_z t = 4.94, R² = 0.115.
  - Entering one session later (R1) gives Sharpe −0.195.
  - Data availability: the 00Z run is complete about 13.0 h before settlement. The 06Z run is out by 07:29 ET and the 12Z run by 13:29 EST.
- **Changes the dev conclusion?** No. It explains the null and meets the "effect only before entry" falsifier.
- **Fix:** none to H1. Report it as a post-hoc diagnostic.
- **Legitimate amendment?** As a dev-only diagnostic, yes. An earlier-clock variant would be a new trial, and this holdout cannot validate it.

**2. P1 misalignment (your known issue ii).** Bug in a diagnostic, verified. `src/signals.py:109-111` uses calendar-day differences; `src/analysis.py:83` uses the nearest pair.

| P1 spec (joint regression) | d_a t | d_p_cold t | d_B t |
|---|---|---|---|
| Repo as published | 1.62 | −0.07 | 1.76 |
| Session-aligned changes | 2.21 | 1.23 | 1.15 |
| Traded pair | 1.25 | −1.03 | 1.07 |
| Both fixes | 1.69 | 0.88 | 0.82 |

- **Single-regressor results:**
  - Session-aligned Δa: t = 2.91. It is robust: HAC(5) gives 2.97, excluding Feb 2021 gives 3.66, leave-one-winter-out ranges 2.14–3.36. On the traded spread t = 2.41–2.87; on outright NG1 t = 4.26.
  - Mondays: t = 3.83–4.17. Regular Tue–Fri sessions: the change is priced the session before (D−2→D−1), t = 2.38–2.57.
  - p_cold changes add nothing beyond the mean (t = 0.80–0.85).
- **Changes the dev conclusion?** The diagnostic's answer changes: mean revisions are priced at or before entry, and ΔB is not robust (0.82–2.05 depending on spec). H1 is unaffected.
- **Fix:** in `same_day_returns`, difference against the previous session's init, and report the traded-pair and Monday/Tue–Fri splits. Keep the original P1 as well.
- **Legitimate amendment?** Yes. P1 is not a §9 criterion, and §7 line 100 already specifies a D−1→D settlement return.

**4. Power, and a holdout that cannot confirm anything.** Statistical limitation, verified with corrections.

- **Changes the dev conclusion?** It changes the framing: report "inconclusive, consistent with 0 and with Sharpe up to about 0.7" (§9 line 121). Since M2 already fails in dev, no holdout outcome can change any §9 decision.

## 3. ChatGPT's five points

**(1) Entry after the reaction; daily data can't isolate it: correct in substance, wrong on mechanism and on what daily data can show.**
- The 00Z run is complete at about 01:30 EST, 13.0 h before settlement. Two newer GEFS cycles are public before the TAS fill.
- The reaction is mostly multi-day, not overnight. Onsets show +1.04σ over D−5..D−1, then only −0.05σ from D−1 to D.
- Changes in the forecast mean are priced same day once aligned: t = 2.9, with Mondays at 3.8–4.2. On Tue–Fri they are priced one session earlier (t = 2.4–2.6).
- Daily data can show the pre-emption. They only cannot locate the intraday timing.

**(2) Rule trades the level, not the change: partly correct as a description; not shown to matter.**
- 24 of 69 trigger days had falling p_cold. But all 17 first entries are fresh crossings relative to the previous session.
- The 4 flat or falling entries are all same-pair re-entries, netting −$13,710 (for example 0.61→0.36 on 2025-01-14, −$4,200).
- Uri (+$26,620) was itself a level re-entry, with prior p_cold 0.97 and a change of only +0.03.
- Within triggers, the change has t = −0.66, and Δp_cold adds nothing same-day (t = 0.85).
- A level rule fits the pre-registered tail-neglect and premium story.

**(3) Spread is not the outright: partly correct.**
- "Both legs rise, spread falls" happens on 13.4% of days but in only 1 of 23 trades.
- Spread and NG1 correlate at 0.638, with beta 0.15. Their signs agree on 20 of 23 trades.
- Cold revisions do reach every near-over-far spread: M1–M2 t = 2.45, M2–M3 t = 3.02, M3–M4 t = 3.66.
- The real problems with the specific pair are the Feb–Mar non-response (difference t = −2.60) and the 6 of 23 M2–M3 trades.
- The outright version (R7) has Sharpe −0.35.

**(4) Horizon mismatch: partly correct.**
- It is wrong about the hold. The price response to realised week-2 weather happens within D→D+5 (t = +2.92 on the traded pair) and not afterwards (D+5→D+10 t = −0.94).
- It is right about delivery: 0 of 23 trades have window overlap with NG1's delivery month, and 88% of window days fall in the month before. This is structural.
- It is right about K/B: their window is never in NG1's delivery month (0 of 516 sessions), it sits inside the hold, and K's reliability is 0.644.

**(5) Edge too weak and Uri-dependent: correct.**
- Mean trade $615, SD $8,627, t = 0.34.
- Without the 2021-02-08 trade the total is −$12,470.
- 90% Sharpe confidence interval [−0.46, 0.68].
- At 1x, H1 (0.073) trails B0 (0.150) and B1 (0.144). At 2x, H1 (0.008) trails B1 (0.085).
- Nuance: with a minimum detectable Sharpe of 1.15, this is "inconclusive", not "no edge".

**Single most useful additional diagnostic:** add the pre-entry event study and lead-lag table to the note.
